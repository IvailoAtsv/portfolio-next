# Keep Up case study

Mode: Experience

Audience: Hiring teams who opened Keep Up from the reel and need to see a real mobile product, not a motion montage.

Job: Understand Keep Up as a daily quiz plus structured learning paths, with the home screen in a phone from the first viewport, then inspect the duck, the path map, and two original quiz recordings.

Primary action: Try Keep Up on the live PWA. Secondary action: Read learning paths, then continue to Sellphy.

Proof: Live home and CI/CD path captures supplied by the author; production mascot sprites (idle, correct, wrong); original quiz recordings for tap-to-build and the +1; facts from the Keep Up repository (15 steps, 4 difficulties, 5 sections per step, SM-2 grades, IndexedDB). No invented metrics.

Visual world: Inherit the studio reel. Frame the app in the same ink iPhone shell used on HLP Labs. Do not film-grain the neo-brutalist UI. Keep captures legible.

First viewport: Title and two-sentence product truth on the left. Tilted iPhone with the live home screen on the right. Meta row: role, product, system, craft.

Interaction: Existing film-gate, mascot sprite player with three state buttons, in-view video playback. No new motion language.

Responsive contract: Desktop keeps heading/phone split. Mobile stacks heading, then phone. Path section reuses the HLP yellow mobile-proof stage and collapses to a column under 1100px.

Success bar: A visitor can name the two tracks (quiz skills vs learning paths), tap three duck states, and see the path map without scrolling past a wall of identical quiz videos.
