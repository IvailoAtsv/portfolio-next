# Portfolio surface brief

Mode: Experience

Audience: Potential employers and hiring teams evaluating product judgment, visual craft, engineering depth, and ownership.

Job: Understand within seconds that Ivaylo designs and builds serious digital products, inspect credible proof in HLP Labs, then continue through the project reel or contact him.

Primary action: Open the HLP Labs case study. Secondary action: Get in touch.

Proof: Real HLP Labs storefront, mobile, AI assistant, and admin captures; factual system scope from the source repository; supporting products Keep Up, Sellphy, and Finance Me. No invented metrics, testimonials, or awards.

Visual world: Early sound-era rubber-hose animation studio, close to the approved Bouncy Beats reference and informed by theatrical 1930s poster staging. Warm paper stock, black ink, gray wash, heavy rounded outlined display type, scalloped and wavy frames, compact pill controls, dimensional cel staging, and one authored film-gate transition. Characters are product objects rather than generic mascots, and every gesture explains a real action. The page should feel drawn and performed, not themed with decorative doodles.

First viewport: A compact studio-mark and pill navigation sit above a large two-line promise on the left. On the right, a finished rubber-hose maker scene stages design and engineering as one act. A concise introduction, two strong actions, three proof seals, and a wavy curtain edge complete the viewport. It must read at 390px without shrinking into a desktop poster.

Interaction: A film-gate entrance and exit, restrained scene parallax, ink-fill button states, project frames that advance like cels without hover scaling, animated object-character gestures, keyboard-visible focus, touch-safe controls, and a complete reduced-motion path. Motion has one rhythmic language instead of identical scroll fades.

Design system: Paper #efe9da, ink #11110f, smoke #77766f, chalk #faf7ed, signal red #c74732, acid yellow #e4c74a. Use Fredoka for authored display lettering and Barlow Condensed for compact editorial copy. Borders are hand-shaped through CSS radii and SVG geometry, usually 2px ink. Depth uses soft low-opacity ink shadows plus small vertical offset, never hard block shadows. Body measure is 65–72ch; display type caps at 6rem.
