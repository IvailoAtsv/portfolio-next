Mode: Experience

Audience: Hiring teams who opened Sellphy from the reel and need to see a real shop platform, not a generic ecommerce card.

Job: Understand Sellphy as statically generated public shops plus a seller dashboard with a first-run tour on each main screen, and take the 83% hosting drop and 25+ hours of monthly maintenance saved as author-stated outcomes.

Primary action: Read static storefronts, the six-step editor, three dressed product pages, tours, and catalog/SEO captures. Secondary action: Continue to Finance Me.

Proof: Author-supplied 2026-09-03 dashboard and storefront captures (builder, DEMO/Alcona/Gymleco product pages, products, page preview, analytics, analytics tour); facts from Cu-store (force-static public routes, revalidatePath/tags, category filter ISR encoding, 17 tour configs, six-step SetupWizard, product-page trust/gallery/quantity, orderMode request labels including calculator). Hosting 83% and 25+ hours/month are author-supplied. Dashboard numbers are demo data, labeled.

Visual world: Inherit the studio reel. Frame dashboard captures in the existing screen-window chrome. Do not film-grain or grayscale the dark UI. Keep captures legible.

First viewport: Title and two-sentence product truth on the left, including the 83% hosting drop. Builder screenshot (step 2 of 6, video hero) on the right. Meta row: role, product, system, scope.

Interaction: Existing film-gate and case-study motion. No new motion language.

Responsive contract: Desktop keeps heading/screenshot split. Mobile stacks heading, then screenshot. Six-step editor list is 3x2, then a single column under 700px. Product-page strip is three columns, then a single column under 1100px. Catalog/SEO pair stacks in one column at all widths.

Success bar: A visitor can name static generation as the hosting move, see three shops sharing one product page, and recognize that dashboard screens start with a tour.
