# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro with Tailwind CSS, deployed on Vercel. The portfolio does not need a CMS.

## Users

The primary audience is potential employers and hiring teams evaluating Ivaylo Atanassov's seniority, product judgment, technical range, and design craft.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio presents Ivaylo as both a developer and a designer. Its job is to demonstrate seniority and craft through real work and help him get hired.

## Positioning

The portfolio should show one practitioner who can move between product thinking, interface design, and full-stack implementation instead of presenting development and design as unrelated disciplines.

## Operating Context

Visitors are likely to arrive with limited time, form an initial judgment quickly, and then inspect selected projects for credible evidence of scope, decisions, execution quality, and ownership.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a focused personal portfolio rather than a freelance-services brochure.
- Development and design work must both be legible without splitting the portfolio into two disconnected identities.
- The core project set is HLP Labs, Keep Up, Sellphy, and potentially Finance Me.
- Project content can live in the repository; no CMS or administrative interface is required.
- The implementation target is Astro with Tailwind CSS on Vercel.
- The existing Next.js portfolio is an anti-reference and will be replaced rather than visually refined.

## Brand Commitments

- The public identity is Ivaylo Atanassov and the portfolio language is English.
- The chosen visual world is an early sound-era rubber-hose animation studio: warm paper, confident black ink, gray wash, rounded display lettering, object-led scenes, a short film-gate transition, and physically authored motion.
- The illustration can be joyful, but the case-study evidence stays direct, specific, and credible for hiring teams.
- Interface captures remain legible and real. Illustration frames the proof; it never replaces it.
- Motion must feel composed as a small animated reel, remain responsive on touch devices, and reduce cleanly when `prefers-reduced-motion` is enabled.

## Product Principles

- Lead with proof of judgment and execution, not a list of technologies.
- Make the relationship between design and development visible in every substantial case study.
- Prefer a few deep, credible projects over a large grid of shallow cards.
- Let artifacts, decisions, and outcomes carry the story instead of self-promotional claims.
- Make the hiring path obvious without turning the site into a sales funnel.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The portfolio should be keyboard usable, responsive from mobile through large desktop, respectful of reduced-motion preferences, and readable with strong contrast and clear focus states.
